# LIM-NO-CROSS-CITY: Comparisons with other cities cannot be made from this data

## What it is

The snapshot contains only Boston's own open data. Claims that compare Boston with other cities
("safest major city in the country") need other cities' data under harmonised definitions.

## Evidence

- No table in the snapshot covers another jurisdiction.
- Even Boston's side of such a comparison cannot be rebuilt to official definitions from this
  data: open-data Part One excludes rape and labels arson "Other" (see LIM-EXCLUDED-OFFENSES and
  LIM-UCR-PART-DEFINITION).
- The "safest major city" claim has been fact-checked (GBH, 2024 and 2025) using external
  comparisons, with contested results.

## How it can change a claim

A cross-city claim may be true or false, but this data cannot establish either.

## What to do

Label cross-city claims `unverifiable`, and say why. A Boston-only component of such a claim
("Boston's homicides fell") can still be checked on its own terms.
