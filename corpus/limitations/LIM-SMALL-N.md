# LIM-SMALL-N: Small counts make percentages and rankings unstable

## What it is

Many claims are about rare events or small areas. With small counts, one or two events change
percentages dramatically and reorder rankings, and chance variation dominates.

## Evidence

- Homicide incidents city-wide range from 22 to 52 a year (2019–2025).
- By district and year, 57 of 61 homicide counts are under 10; the median is 2.
- Fatal shooting victims range from 19 to 48 a year (2015–2025).

## How it can change a claim

A change from 22 to 24 is a 9% increase from two events. Neighborhood rankings built on counts
this small are mostly noise, and the sign of a year-over-year change can flip from one year to
the next without any underlying change.

## What to do

Report the counts behind any percentage. Prefer longer windows or larger areas for rare events.
Do not rank areas on counts under 10; this project suppresses cells under 10 in published
output.
